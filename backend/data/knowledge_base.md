
# FitNova AI Clinical Knowledge Base: Nutrition & Exercise Guidelines

## Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) and Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

### Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) Estimation

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor Equation, the clinical standard endorsed by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics (ADA) for its high accuracy in non-clinical adult populations.

For Males:


$$BMR = (10 \times \text{Weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{Height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{Age in years}) + 5$$

For Females:


$$BMR = (10 \times \text{Weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{Height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{Age in years}) - 161$$

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**
Calculates baseline energy requirement in **kcal/day** at rest.

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics (ADA) Evidence Analysis Library; Mifflin et al., 1990.

**Boundary Conditions**

* **Obesity:** Extremely high body fat percentages may cause Mifflin-St Jeor to overestimate BMR. In cases of clinical obesity, using Lean Body Mass (LBM) via the Katch-McArdle formula is preferred if body fat percentage is known.
* **South Asian Phenotype:** South Asian populations often possess lower lean muscle mass and higher visceral fat at identical BMIs compared to Caucasian populations, potentially resulting in a naturally lower actual BMR than formula estimates.

### Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**


$$TDEE = BMR \times \text{Activity Factor (AF)}$$

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

| Activity Level | Activity Factor (AF) Multiplier | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sedentary | **1.2** | Little to no exercise, desk job |
| Lightly Active | **1.375** | Light exercise/sports 1-3 days/week |
| Moderately Active | **1.55** | Moderate exercise/sports 3-5 days/week |
| Very Active | **1.725** | Hard exercise/sports 6-7 days/week |
| Extra Active | **1.9** | Very hard daily exercise/sports & physical job |

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
World Health Organization (WHO) Energy Requirements; Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO).

**Boundary Conditions**

* **Caloric Deficit (Fat Loss):** Recommend subtracting **300 - 500 kcal/day** from TDEE.
* **Caloric Surplus (Hypertrophy):** Recommend adding **250 - 500 kcal/day** to TDEE.

## Macronutrient Guidelines for Hypertrophy and Body Composition

### Protein Requirements for Muscle Growth (Hypertrophy)

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Target daily protein intake scaled to total body weight, measured in **g/kg/day**.

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

| Fitness Goal / Population | Recommended Daily Protein Target |
| --- | --- |
| General Sedentary Adult | **0.8 g/kg/day** |
| Endurance Athletes | **1.2 - 1.4 g/kg/day** |
| Muscle Hypertrophy / Resistance Training | **1.6 - 2.2 g/kg/day** |
| Fat Loss with Lean Mass Retention | **2.3 - 3.1 g/kg/day** (Fat-Free Mass) |

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN) Position Stand: Protein and Exercise (2017); American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM).

**Boundary Conditions**

* **Per-Meal Threshold:** Muscle protein synthesis maximizes at an intake of **20 - 40 g** (or **0.25 - 0.40 g/kg/meal**) of high-quality protein per meal.
* **Plant-Based Diets:** Individuals relying on plant-based proteins (common in South Asian/Pakistani diets such as lentils and chickpeas) should target the upper end of the range (**2.0 - 2.2 g/kg/day**) due to lower Digestible Indispensable Amino Acid Scores (DIAAS) and lower leucine content compared to animal proteins.

### Carbohydrate and Dietary Fat Distribution

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Percentage of total **kcal** and **g/kg/day**.

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

| Macronutrient | Target Range |
| --- | --- |
| Dietary Fats | **20% - 35%** of total TDEE; Minimum **1.0 g/kg/day** for hormonal health |
| Carbohydrates | Remainder of calories post-protein and fat allocation; **3.0 - 5.0 g/kg/day** for standard resistance training |

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
USDA Dietary Guidelines for Americans (2020-2025); ACSM.

**Boundary Conditions**

* **Fat Intake Minimum:** Dietary fat should not drop below **20%** of total **kcal**, as severe restriction impairs testosterone and estrogen synthesis.

## Physical Activity and Cardiovascular Health Metrics

### Daily Step Targets

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Total ambulatory motion measured in **steps/day**.

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

| Goal / Demographic | Recommended Daily Target |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline Health / Sedentary Avoidance | **7,000 - 8,000 steps/day** |
| General Cardiovascular Health / Mortality Reduction | **8,000 - 10,000 steps/day** |
| Older Adults (Age 60+) | **6,000 - 8,000 steps/day** |

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
National Institutes of Health (NIH) Epidemiology Studies (2022); World Health Organization (WHO) Guidelines on Physical Activity.

**Boundary Conditions**

* **Mortality Plateau:** All-cause mortality risk reduction plateaus at approximately **8,000 - 10,000 steps/day** for adults under 60. Additional steps provide functional and caloric benefits but do not exponentially decrease mortality risk.
* **Cadence:** Moderate-to-vigorous intensity translates to a stepping cadence of approximately **100 steps/minute**.

### Aerobic and Anaerobic Clinical Minimums

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Duration of activity per week measured in **minutes/week** and **days/week**.

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

* **Aerobic:** **150 - 300 minutes/week** of moderate-intensity OR **75 - 150 minutes/week** of vigorous-intensity.
* **Resistance/Strength:** **$\ge$ 2 days/week** targeting all major muscle groups.

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
WHO Guidelines on Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour (2020).

**Boundary Conditions**

* **South Asian Genetic Risk:** South Asians have a heightened genetic predisposition to cardiovascular diseases and insulin resistance. The Pakistan Endocrine Society supports strictly adhering to the upper bounds (**300 minutes/week**) for this demographic to optimize glycemic control.

## South Asian Specific Anthropometric Cut-Offs

### Body Mass Index (BMI) Revisions

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**


$$BMI = \frac{\text{Weight in kg}}{(\text{Height in m})^2}$$

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**
Due to higher cardiometabolic risk at lower body weights, the WHO established modified BMI classifications for Asian populations.

| Classification | Global Standard BMI (**kg/m²**) | Asian/South Asian Standard BMI (**kg/m²**) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | **18.5 - 24.9** | **18.5 - 22.9** |
| Overweight | **25.0 - 29.9** | **23.0 - 24.9** |
| Obese | **$\ge$ 30.0** | **$\ge$ 25.0** |

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
WHO Expert Consultation on Appropriate BMI for Asian Populations (2004); Pakistan Medical Association (PMA).

**Boundary Conditions**

* BMI is a population-level screening tool and cannot distinguish between fat-free mass (muscle) and adipose tissue. Athletes or heavy resistance trainees may flag as overweight/obese despite low body fat percentages. It must be paired with waist circumference metrics.


## Hydration and Electrolyte Balance

### Daily Fluid Intake Requirements

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Total daily water intake scaled to body weight and caloric expenditure, measured in **liters/day** or **ml/kcal**.

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

| Population / Condition | Recommended Fluid Target |
| --- | --- |
| General Adult Baseline (Women) | **2.7 liters/day** total water from all beverages and foods |
| General Adult Baseline (Men) | **3.7 liters/day** total water from all beverages and foods |
| Baseline Scaled Formula | **30 - 35 ml/kg/day** |
| Exercise Hydration Add-On | **400 - 800 ml** per hour of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity |

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
Institute of Medicine (IOM) Dietary Reference Intakes for Water; American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) Position Stand on Exercise and Fluid Replacement.

**Boundary Conditions**

* **Environmental Stress:** High ambient temperatures and humidity (such as in tropical or sub-tropical South Asian climates) increase sweat rates by **1.0 - 2.0 liters/hour**, requiring electrolyte replacement (sodium chloride and potassium) to prevent hyponatremia and exercise-associated muscle cramps.

## Muscle Recovery, DOMS, and Sleep Optimization

### Sleep Duration for Hypertrophy and Neuromuscular Recovery

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Continuous nocturnal sleep duration measured in **hours/night**.

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

* **Active Adults / Resistance Trainees:** **7.5 - 9.0 hours/night** to optimize Growth Hormone (GH) secretion, tissue repair, and muscle protein synthesis (MPS).

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
National Sleep Foundation; International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN).

**Boundary Conditions**

* **Sleep Deprivation:** Chronic restriction (<6 hours/night) elevates systemic cortisol, suppresses nocturnal testosterone synthesis, decreases muscle glycogen storage capacity, and significantly prolongs recovery from Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness (DOMS).

### Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness (DOMS) Management

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Temporal management of structural muscle microtrauma occurring **24 to 72 hours** post-exercise.

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

| Recovery Modality | Clinical Protocol |
| --- | --- |
| Active Recovery | Low-intensity aerobic exercise (e.g., walking, cycling at <= 50% VO2 max) for **20 - 30 minutes** |
| Protein Co-Ingestion | **0.25 - 0.40 g/kg** of high-quality protein every 3–5 hours |

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
ACSM; Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research (JSCR).

## Resistance Training Prescription & Progressive Overload

### Volume, Intensity, and Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE)

**Standard Unit / Formula Definition**
Quantified via Repetitions in Reserve (RIR) and Rating of Perceived Exertion (RPE) on a 1–10 scale.

**Clinical Target / Numerical Value Range**

| Training Metric | Clinical Benchmark |
| --- | --- |
| Hypertrophy Intensity | **RPE 7 to 10** (or **0 to 3 RIR** — meaning 0 to 3 repetitions shy of technical failure) |
| Weekly Volume Landmark | **10 - 20 working sets per muscle group per week** for optimal hypertrophic gains |

**Primary Source / Consensus Body Citation**
National Strength and Conditioning Association (NSCA); Schoenfeld et al., Meta-analyses on Resistance Training Volume and Frequency.

**Boundary Conditions**

* **Progressive Overload Principle:** To prevent neuromuscular adaptation plateaus, mechanical tension must increase incrementally by **2.5% - 10% load per week** or when the upper rep range threshold is comfortably met with proper form.
